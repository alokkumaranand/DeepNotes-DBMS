YOUR BROWSER, A LITTLE MORE THOUGHTFUL

Make room for DeepNotes.

A side panel for the things you want to keep. Your local backend does the organizing.

01

Connect your extension

Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, then Load unpacked → select DeepNotes/chrome-extension. Pin DeepNotes in Chrome's Extensions menu.

Copy its 32-character ID from that page or the panel's Settings. Stop the old backend, then open PowerShell in the DeepNotes application directory and run:

$env:DEEPNOTES_EXTENSION_ID = "PASTE_YOUR_EXTENSION_ID_HERE"
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m uvicorn deepnotes.extension_api:create_app --factory --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8000

Keep your existing model keys in the backend's .env file, never in Chrome. To remember the extension ID, you may append DEEPNOTES_EXTENSION_ID=your_id to that .env yourself.

02

Keep it beside your tab

Click the pinned DeepNotes icon. The panel stays alongside your browser as you work. If it opens on the left, choose Right in Chrome Settings → Appearance → side-panel position. Chrome owns this placement setting; names may vary by version.

Drag the divider to resize. Suggested shortcut: Alt + Shift + N, configurable at chrome://extensions/shortcuts. Chrome 116+ is required.

03

Capture. Ask. Connect.

  • Capture: write a note, attach the current page/selection, or right-click selected text → Send selection to DeepNotes. Review the preview, then Save.
  • Ask: get the curated answer first, with references below. Reopen previous answers in Ask history.
  • Library: inspect interpretations and add missing context. Settings also supports section/global guidance.

Only selected text and page metadata are captured—not the whole page. Nothing is saved automatically. Your source stays fixed when tabs change.

A few things worth knowing

  • The backend must remain running. Only one backend process may use the database.
  • Use the extension-aware launcher above, not the original website-only command.
  • After navigating or changing tabs, click the toolbar icon on the desired tab again to grant temporary capture access.
  • Restricted Chrome pages may block capture; manual notes still work.
  • Drafts stay in local extension storage for this Chrome window. No history monitoring or Chrome sync.
  • If a save times out, check Library before retrying. The backend may already have saved it.
  • For job retries or structure rollback, use the existing local website. The extension keeps daily work simple.
Full installation, privacy, troubleshooting, and test instructions: DeepNotes/chrome-extension/README.md in your project. The extension adds to the existing application; it does not replace your stored notes.
